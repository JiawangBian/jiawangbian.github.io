CV

I am a Nanyang Assistant Professor in the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), where I lead the Spatial Intelligence Lab. My research focuses on spatial intelligence: enabling AI to perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world, with applications in autonomous driving, AR/VR, and robotics.

Previously, I was a Research Scientist at ByteDance Seed, and a postdoctoral researcher at MBZUAI and the University of Oxford. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Adelaide in 2022, supervised by Prof. Ian Reid and Prof. Chunhua Shen, and my B.Eng. from Nankai University in 2016, supervised by Prof. Ming-Ming Cheng. I have published over 45 papers in top venues including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, TPAMI, and IJCV, with over 7,000 citations and an h-index of 23.
